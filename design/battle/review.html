Battle plan prompt

Each row below is one change, shown in three builds of the same game: Guild fighting Atreides in Habbanya Sietch, with Advanced Combat on and 2 forces dialled.

The rows are numbered, so you can reply with the ones to keep or drop. A red tag means the change also affects prompts other than the battle plan.

Original forkcommit 8f203621
Beforethe fork's redesign, as of today
Afterthis change

1.Opponent and aggressor linebattle only

"Opponent forces" and "Aggressor" become small uppercase labels. Aggressor was orange, the warning colour, and is now neutral. When you are the aggressor it says "You" instead of "You are aggressor."

2.Labels above the steppersbattle only

Each stepper gets a label: "Forces", the special force's name when you have them, "Spice", and "From ally" when an ally pays. Without labels the only difference between the forces and spice steppers is the icon.

3.Dial on its own linebattle only

The arrow and white circle become a gold box labelled "Dial", on its own line under the steppers.

In the original, both steppers and the dial fit on one line. The fork's redesign made the steppers wider, so the dial already wrapped to a second line in "Before," with the arrow sitting higher than the number. Change 3 makes that second line deliberate. If you'd rather keep the dial on the stepper line, the other fix is to make the steppers narrower again.

Same shots as row 2.

4.Half-strength hintbattle only

With Advanced Combat on, forces without spice support count half. When that lowers the dial, a line under it explains why: "2 forces count at half strength without Spice support." Neither earlier build explains why 2 forces give a dial of 1. You can see the hint at the bottom of the row 2 "After" shot.

5.Section labels and spacingbattle only

The leaders get a "Leader" label, which they never had. Leader, Weapon and Defense use the same small label as the steppers and sit 16px apart, where every block used to be 4px apart.

6.Gap between rows of imagesevery image picker

In both earlier builds, when the images wrap to a second row it touches the first. They now sit 6px apart, and 4px side by side (previously 4px of right padding). This is SelectFromImageComponent, which is also used for card and leader choices in other prompts. I haven't screenshotted those. See the leader row in row 5.

7.Stepper button fontevery number stepper

The − and + buttons switch from the display font to the body font, because the display font draws the minus sign with little serifs, like a bracket. This is .num in _number.scss, used by every stepper. The original didn't have these buttons; it had arrow icons.
