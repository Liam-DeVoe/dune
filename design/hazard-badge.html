Corner badge variants

The hazard chip keeps its icon and its word. A small badge on the icon says which way the word goes: a check for Protected, a mark for Exposed. In storm keeps its red fill and no badge, since the fill already says it. Each variant is shown in the four popovers at real size, then the four chip states magnified so the badge can be judged.

Recommendation: 3, mark only the danger. One red badge on Exposed, nothing on Protected. It is the least ink for the same information, and the safe state is the one that needs no reassurance. If you want both states marked, 1 is the cleanest of the two-badge variants and 6 is 1 with the chip edge agreeing.
