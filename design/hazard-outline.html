Solid outline variants

Variant 10 from the badge page, taken further. Every chip keeps its icon and word at full opacity. Protected gets a solid sand outline, Exposed a solid storm-red one, and In storm keeps its red fill. The variants below change one thing each: the weight, one of the two colours, what else picks up the colour, or how the outline is built.

Recommendation: 4, Protected in rock. Sand is one step from gold, and gold outlines mean "selected" everywhere else in the client. Rock is the same warm family a step down, so Protected is clearly outlined and clearly not a highlight, while the red on Exposed stays the loudest thing in the popover short of the In storm fill. If you want the storm glyph to carry colour too, 7 stacks on top of any of these.

1.5px is the middle, but a 1x display has no half pixel: Chrome draws it as 1px on one edge and 2px on another, so the two chips in a popover can come out mismatched. 2px and 1px are honest at any scale.
