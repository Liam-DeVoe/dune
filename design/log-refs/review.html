Log references review: sectors, factions, spice

Every message the client renders now names a sector or territory with a place chip and a faction with a faction chip. Hovering a place chip lights its sectors on the map. Hovering a faction chip tints every sector holding their forces and marks their traitor in your panel. Every spice amount is its digits on the harvester icon.

The fixture: turn 3, seen as the Bene Gesserit. Their traitor is Piter de Vries, a Harkonnen leader, and the report names the Harkonnen twice. Two of the referenced places sit in multi-sector territories.

Calls I made that you should approve or change

  • Every rendered message got the chips: the phase report, chat, the prompt on the act card, and deal text share one renderer. Chips on the parchment act card take the on-parchment colours.
  • Your own faction is named like any other, in gold, with the ordinary border. The gold border is reserved for the hovered chip.
  • A territory chip lights every sector of the territory. A homeworld or a place with no outline on the map lights nothing and keeps only its tooltip.
  • The faction tint is the faction colour at 30 percent, drawn with the map's own hover shape. The tint follows where their forces are at the moment you hover, not where they were when the line was written.
  • Routes are recorded where the line is written. A message can now carry where forces came from and went to, set by moves, shipments, worm rides and accompanying shipments in the shared game code. The report tags such lines and the hover script draws the arrow. Hovering a chip inside a journey line lights that chip's sector and still draws the line's arrow.
  • The traitor mark is a gold ring on the card, and face dancers of that faction get the same ring.
  • Chips and journey lines show the pointer cursor, as the hover card popovers already do. A chip inside a journey line lights only its own sector; the arrow appears only with the mouse on the line's text.
  • Spice: the harvester with digits is now used in the log, the player panel, the Mentat notes, and the territory popover (both the spice on the ground and "may contain N"). The current bid keeps its disc-backed harvester, and the map tokens and number pickers are unchanged. The deal badges next to "Deals" are counts of offers, not spice, so they stay.
  • The spice pill is gone from the app CSS and from the design language, where every example now uses the harvester form.
  • Three-digit amounts overflow the icon at log size. Nothing in the fixtures reaches that; it would need a wider glyph or a smaller face.
