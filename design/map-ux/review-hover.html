Map UX review: sector hover

The sector under the mouse now gets a light white wash and a thin white outline, clipped to the sector wedge like the action tints. It is the same in every phase and says nothing about whether the sector is a valid choice. Screenshots are from the local client, Fremen on turn 1 for the move shots and Emperor for the shipment shot. The cursor is over Pasty Mesa South in the first two pairs.

Calls I made that you should approve or change

  1. The hover layer sits under the action tints. Selectable, target and selected sectors keep their look; the hover shows through as a lighter wash. In the shipment phase this makes the hover faint, since every sector is a target. Putting it above the tints, or raising the wash, would make it read more but also muddy the faction colours.
  2. The outline is 2 map units wide, drawn as a 4 unit stroke clipped to the sector so only the inner half shows, the same trick as the action tints. Wash is white at 0.18.
  3. Force and player tokens end the hover. They sit above the map with their own popovers, so moving onto a token clears the sector shape, the same as it hides the sector's intel popover.
  4. Intel popovers are only switched off while a panel is pinned, since that is the only time they cover something. Selecting a source no longer hides them.
  5. Homeworlds, the hidden mobile stronghold and discovered locations get no hover shape. They have no territory border in the skin.
  6. The hover is pure JavaScript. The shapes are rendered once by Blazor, hidden, and switched on by class from delegated mouseover and mouseout listeners on the map. Nothing goes through Blazor on a mouse move.
