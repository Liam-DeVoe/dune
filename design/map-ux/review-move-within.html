Map UX review: moving within a territory

Forces may move from one sector of a territory to another. The map-based move flow made that unreachable: a click on a sector of the source territory either joined the sources, when the sector held your forces, or was ignored, when it was empty. Now an empty sector pins as the destination, and a sector that holds your forces asks which of the two you meant.

The fixture: Fremen with 4 forces and a Fedaykin in Pasty Mesa (Far North) and 3 forces in Pasty Mesa (North). Pasty Mesa (South) is empty. Every shot starts with Far North clicked as the source.

Before: the click silently added North as a second source, and no sequence of clicks could make it the destination. After: the map pins a choice at North. "Add as source" does what the old click did. "Move here" makes North the destination.

Calls I made that you should approve or change

  • The choice only appears when both readings are possible. A sector of the source territory with none of your forces pins straight away. A sector with your forces in a territory you have not selected still pins straight away, as before.
  • The choice panel sits where the pinned panel would, with the arrow already drawn from the sources to the clicked sector. Clicking anywhere else on the map dismisses it and handles that click normally. Escape and right click cancel the whole selection, as they already did.
  • Button wording: "Add as source" and "Move here". The primary style is on "Move here", since moving is what a click on a destination usually means.
