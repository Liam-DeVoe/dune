Map UX review: where the pinned panel sits

The panel pinned to a destination used to hang above the bounding box of the whole arrow, centred between the source and the destination. With a long arrow that put it far from both ends, with the caret pointing at empty map. Now it hangs above the destination, in line with it. A source token that would be hidden under the panel pushes the panel up until the token shows. Near the top of the map the panel goes below the destination, as before.

Every map panel goes through the same rule: move, ship, setup placement and the map pickers.

Calls I made that you should approve or change

  • The arrow runs behind the panel on a long arrow. The panel sits above the destination whatever the arrow does, so a source above the destination sends the arrow under the panel, and only the head shows. The alternative is to shift the panel sideways off the arrow, which moves the caret off the destination again.
  • The panel's size is a constant guess. Blazor does not know the rendered size when it picks the anchor. The rule assumes a panel up to 220 wide and 110 tall in map units. The move panel measures about 200 by 100, so a source token that just misses the guess could still be partly covered by a taller panel, such as the ally warning.
  • Before a destination is picked, a panel on the sources alone, such as the Guild's "to reserves" choice, still hangs above the sources as before.
