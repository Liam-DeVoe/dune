Map UX review: initial deployment

Starting forces are now placed on the map. A click on an outlined sector stages one force there, shift-click stages one special force, and a right-click (or ctrl-click on a Mac, or a long press on a touch screen) takes the most recent one back. Staged forces are drawn on the board as faded tokens with a dashed ring until you confirm. The sidebar keeps the header, a one-line instruction with three click hints under it, a short list of what is staged with plus and minus buttons, and Confirm. Screenshots are from the local client: the Fremen placing their ten forces on turn 0, and a host placing every faction's forces under the custom setup rule.

Calls I made that you should approve or change

  1. The click hints are three icon lines under "Use the map to place forces.": mouse icon, "place", force token; shift + mouse icon, "place", special force token; right mouse icon, "remove force". The second line only appears for factions that can place special forces.
  2. The Fremen header counts down with every click, plus and minus, and reads "All forces placed." at zero, at which point the red validation line is gone. The host header never had a count, so it is unchanged.
  3. Right-click takes back the most recent force in that sector, whichever type it was. The minus buttons in the list take back the most recent one of that type.
  4. Clicks beyond the limit are ignored. You cannot stage more regular or special forces than the faction holds in reserve, and the Fremen cannot stage more than ten in total. Once a limit is reached, map clicks do nothing and the plus buttons in the list are disabled.
  5. The staging list is a fallback. It only lists sectors with at least one staged force, and shows a stepper for each force type the faction can place, even when that type's count is zero, so a Fedaykin can still be added from the list.
  6. Staged tokens carry the faction being set up, not the host's faction. The old component tagged the battalions with the host's faction; the event ignores the tag, so what is sent still executes the same way.
  7. White cannot stage special forces in the custom setup. Their special force is the No-Field token, which is not placed like this. The old panel hid the hint for White but still accepted the ctrl-click.
  8. Starting spice resets to zero when the host moves on to the next faction. The old component kept the previous faction's value.
  9. The hidden mobile stronghold is still a valid custom setup target because the old component offered it too, but it has no sector on the map, so nothing is outlined for it and it can only be reached by clicking its token when it is on the board.
  10. The context menu is suppressed on sectors so right-click reaches the game. The rest of the page still has its normal menu.
