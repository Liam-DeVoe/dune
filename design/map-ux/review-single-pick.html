Map UX review: single-pick actions

Every action that asks for one location or territory now works the same way: when its panel opens, every valid choice is outlined on the map. Clicking one pins it and shows a small panel next to it with the action's remaining inputs and its confirm button. Clicking another valid place re-pins there; clicking the pinned one again unpins. The sidebar keeps the header, one line saying to use the map, and Pass where the action had one. Converted: retreat, Bene Gesserit accompanying a shipment, flipping advisors to fighters, moving the Hidden Mobile Stronghold (normal and with Karama), sending the worm (normal and with Karama), the Brown move prevention and force removal, placing an ambassador, placing terror, the sneak attack target when terror is revealed, where revived forces land (revival, Raise Dead, and the Purple Nexus card), the Pink and Cyan Nexus betrayal targets, the setup placements for Bene Gesserit, Cyan and the Hidden Mobile Stronghold, entering a discovered stronghold, and the Yellow and Orange ambassador effects.

Calls I made that you should approve or change

  1. A territory is picked by clicking any of its sectors. Only the clicked sector gets the pinned style; the territory's other sectors keep the plain outline. Clicking another sector of the pinned territory unpins.
  2. The ambassador choice stays in the sidebar when placing an ambassador. The ambassador images are large and the choice is independent of the stronghold. The pinned panel shows the chosen ambassador and the confirm button.
  3. The terror token type moved into the pinned panel as a row of small buttons, with the token's description under it. Removing a token (Cyan at high threshold in Collection) names no stronghold, so that case keeps its old sidebar picker and does not touch the map.
  4. Retreat starts with every undialed force selected, trimmed to what the leader allows, like a move starts at the maximum. Passing still has to name a location because the event insists on one; the first valid one is used.
  5. Force counts live in the pinned panel for retreat, the sneak attack, entering a discovered stronghold, and the Yellow and Orange ambassadors. Entering a discovered stronghold starts with all adjacent forces; the Orange ambassador starts with one force.
  6. Brown's force removal shows the factions present in the pinned location as a row of buttons, plus a normal / special toggle when the victim has both kinds there.
  7. Partial panels keep their sidebar confirm. Revival, Raise Dead, the Nexus card and terror revealed only need the map for one input, so the sidebar keeps its confirm button and the pinned panel offers the same confirm. These only take the map while the location matters (for instance once some revived forces are set to land on the planet), and hand it back otherwise, so a shipment or move open at the same time is not blocked.
  8. The Yellow ambassador is a mini move: click the sector to move from (several sectors of one territory add up), then the destination. The counters appear in the pinned panel. The old ctrl-click source selection is gone.
  9. Entering a discovered stronghold gained a "Don't enter" button. The event already supported passing; without a dropdown that auto-selected the only stronghold there was no way to decline.
  10. Homeworlds are not clickable (the skin draws them next to the player token, not as sectors). Brown's force removal and the Orange ambassador can in principle target a homeworld under the Homeworlds rule; those choices are silently unavailable on the map. No converted action has only homeworlds as choices.
  11. The pinned panel redraws on every render of its panel, so a sidebar change (a different ambassador, a different terror type in the remove case) shows up in the pin without a click.
  12. Two converted actions open at once: the first to render takes the map; the other shows its fixed line and Pass and claims the map as soon as it is free.
