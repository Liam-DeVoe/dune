Map UX review: the worm ride

The Fremen now ride Shai-Hulud on the map, the same way they move: click the sector holding the forces that ride, then click where they ride to. The counters and the Ride button sit in the panel pinned to the arrow, and the sidebar only names the worm and offers Pass. Screenshots are from the local client, playing Fremen on turn 3 after the worm appeared in Red Chasm, where they hold 4 forces and 1 Fedaykin. The last section shows the Great Maker from the expansion, which is ridden from reserves.

Calls I made that you should approve or change

  1. Counters start at the maximum for a ride from the board, as the old pickers and the move do. The Great Maker ride starts at one force, as a shipment does, since bringing every reserve is not the common case.
  2. Destinations light up after the source is clicked for a ride from the board, even though the rules do not make them depend on the forces. Outlining them from the start would invite clicking a destination first and then being told there are no forces selected. For the Great Maker there is no source, so they are outlined from the start.
  3. The sidebar is reduced to the header, one line and Pass. The header still names the worm's territory, or the Great Maker.
  4. Several sectors. Clicking another sector of the worm's territory adds it, as a move does. Clicking a sector in another territory replaces the selection. With a Nexus Cunning card (riding from anywhere when you hold nothing in the worm's territory) the old picker let one ride mix forces from several territories; on the map that is one territory per ride.
  5. The ride claims the map as soon as it opens. The ride phase offers Fremen no other action, so nothing else competes for the map. If something ever did, the ride would take the map back as soon as it is released.
  6. The confirm button says Ride! through a new label hook on the shared move component. Move and Hajr keep their labels.
  7. The ride now shares all of the move component's map code. The old opt-out that let it keep the list picker is gone, together with the dead list-picker branch.
