Map UX review: movement and shipment

The move is now made on the map. The sidebar panel only says what to click next and offers Pass. Screenshots are from the local client, playing Fremen on turn 1 with forces in False Wall South and False Wall West. Fremen also hold a Hajr card, so a Hajr panel and a Move panel are both open.

After: the sequence

Click a source sector. It gets a solid outline, every valid destination gets a white dashed outline, and an arrow follows the cursor.

Shipment: calls I made that you should approve or change

  1. The panel opens with one force selected, so a common shipment is two clicks and Ship. Movement opens at the maximum because moving everything is the common case; shipping everything is not.
  2. Options only appear when they apply, inside the panel: White's No-Field choice (and the Cunning second No-Field), the free smuggled force, which homeworld to ship from when there are several, the Karama card for Guild rates, a spice counter for what your ally pays, and the ally-presence warning.
  3. White as secret ally is a Normal / secret ally toggle at the top of the panel when it applies.
  4. Homeworlds are not clickable. They are drawn next to the player token rather than as sectors, so shipping to another faction's homeworld (Homeworlds expansion rule) is not offered on the map yet.
  5. Clicking the selected source again drops it and returns to shipping from reserves. Cancel in the panel does the same.
  6. The panel stays inside the map near the left and right edges, so its caret can point a little off centre.
  7. Karama shipment prevention leaves nothing outlined and the sidebar says why, with Pass.

Calls I made that you should approve or change

  1. Hajr is opt-in. The regular move owns the map. The Hajr panel is one button, "Play Hajr for an extra move first". While the Hajr has the map, the Move panel says to finish or cancel it first.
  2. Several sources. Clicking another sector of the same territory adds it as a second source, since the rules allow that. Clicking a sector in a different territory replaces the selection, unless Planetology is active, in which case it is added as the second territory. The pinned panel then shows one row per source with the sector name.
  3. Counters start at the maximum, as the old pickers did.
  4. Hover intel is switched off on the map while a source is selected. Without this, the popover for the sector you just clicked covers the pinned panel.
  5. Bene Gesserit get a small Fighters / Advisors toggle inside the pinned panel when both are allowed. Not shown here.
  6. The ally warning (forces destroyed at end of move if your ally is there) is shown inside the pinned panel, in red.
  7. Cancel in the pinned panel clears the whole selection. There is no Escape key handling yet.
  8. The worm ride (Fremen riding Shai-Hulud) shares this component but keeps its old sidebar picker for now. It will get the same treatment in a later step.
