Battle headings

Built: 3, face-off. In the client each faction's name sits under its sigil, so long names like Bene Gesserit fit, and a long territory name wraps. The heading's tooltip gives the result. The engine no longer logs "X initiate battle against Y in Z", since the heading says it.

In the built panel each battle opens with "Atreides vs Bene Gesserit in Arrakeen" made of the same chips as the log lines, so it reads as one more line. Three ways to make the heading read as the heading of the whole battle. Each panel shows the same two fights: one Atreides won, and one still going.

1 · Band

A tinted strip with the territory as the title, small sigils for the two sides, and the result as a pill at the right. The lines below sit inside the battle with no rule. Strongest grouping.

2 · Titled rule

Same form as the phase headings in the design language: the territory in the label face, a hairline, the result at the far end. The two sides go on a quiet line under it. Lightest, and it doesn't add a new visual element to the panel.

3 · Face-off

The two sides facing each other with large sigils, the territory between them. The winner's sigil gets a gold ring and the loser fades. Reads best at a glance, costs the most height.
