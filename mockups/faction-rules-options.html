Where should a faction's full rules open?

Each box below is a working mockup. Hover a faction token on the map edge to see its popover, then try the interaction listed for that option. The faction, leaders and rules text are placeholders. The popover styling follows .pop-faction in _pop.scss.

A. Side column panel current

Hover for the summary. Click the token and the full sheet opens at the top of the side column.

The sheet stays open while you play, so you can keep reading it.
It opens on the far side of the screen, so nothing ties it to the token you clicked.
It pushes the players list down.
The hover popover covers the map while the panel opens, and you then have to look across the screen.

B. Full sheet in the hover popover always big

Hover shows everything: the summary and the full rules. Clicking does nothing.

No extra interaction to learn.
A huge popover appears every time the mouse crosses a token, covering much of the map.
You can't scroll it. It closes as soon as the mouse leaves the token.
The short summary, which is what you want most of the time, gets buried.

C. Hold Shift to expand

Hover for the summary. Hold Shift and the same popover grows into the full sheet. Let go and it shrinks back.

The full sheet grows out of the same popover, so it clearly belongs to that token.
Quick for experienced players.
Hard to discover without a hint, and there's no Shift on touch screens.
You have to keep holding a key and keep the mouse still while you read.
Still can't scroll, because moving the mouse into the popover closes it.

D. Click to pin and expand in place recommended

Hover for the summary. Click and the popover stays put, grows into the full sheet, and scrolls. Close it with ×, Esc, a click elsewhere, or another click on the token.

Opens right where you were looking, out of the popover you were already reading.
Scrollable, stays open, and works on touch.
The existing "Click for full rules" footer already tells people how to get there.
Covers part of the map while it's open (but only until you close it).
Needs a rule for when the game opens its own pinned panel on the map. Simplest: only one pin at a time.

E. Click to open a centered dialog

Hover for the summary. Click opens the full sheet in a dialog in the middle of the screen, over a dimmed backdrop.

The most room for reading, and it's obvious how to close it.
Blocks the whole game while open, which is heavy for a quick check.
Like A, it moves away from the token, though the dimmed backdrop makes the jump less confusing.
