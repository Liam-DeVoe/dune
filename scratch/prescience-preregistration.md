# Prescience: what should happen (written before reading the implementation)

Rule basis (Atreides, 2019 rules): before battle plans are made, Atreides may make
their opponent reveal one element of the plan they will use: leader, weapon, defense
or dial. If the answer is "no weapon" / "no defense", Atreides can't ask for
something else. The revealed element is binding. Atreides then makes their own plan
knowing it. Alliance: Atreides may use prescience in an ally's battle. Atreides
Karma: see the opponent's entire plan. Voice and prescience can both be used in one
battle; Voice comes first.

Actors: G = whoever benefits from prescience (Atreides, or Atreides' ally in their own
battle). O = G's opponent.

| # | Scenario | Should G learn the element? | When | Can G (re)make their plan with it? | Can O change the element? |
|---|----------|-----------------------------|------|------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| 1 | Prescience used, neither has submitted | yes | as soon as O submits | yes, G hasn't submitted yet | no, once submitted it's binding |
| 2 | G submitted before using prescience / before O submitted | yes | when O submits | yes, G must be able to revise until plans are revealed, or the ability is worthless | no |
| 3 | Prescience used after O already submitted | yes | immediately | yes | no |
| 4 | O revises their plan after G saw the element | G sees the current value | on each revision | yes | O may change other elements, not the revealed one |
| 5 | Answer is "no weapon"/"no defense" | yes, G sees "none" | when O submits | yes | O can't add a weapon/defense later |
| 6 | Dial asked, O's dial includes contributions (e.g. Ecaz) or half-strength forces | yes, the dial O's plan actually gives | when O submits | yes | no |
| 7 | Ally battle, Atreides shares prescience (permission on) | yes, the ally (the one planning) must see it; Atreides may also see it | when O submits | yes, ally can revise | no |
| 8 | Ally battle, permission off | prescience not available | – | – | – |
| 9 | Atreides is not in the battle and not allied to a combatant | not available | – | – | – |
| 10 | Prescience prevented by Karma (opponent's Karma) | no | – | – | – |
| 11 | Prescience cancelled by a Nexus betrayal after being used | no, info removed | – | G should still be allowed to revise | – |
| 12 | Voice and prescience in the same battle | yes | when O submits | yes | Voice constraints on O still apply |
| 13 | Atreides Karma: whole plan | G sees every element | when O submits | yes | no |
| 14 | Nexus cunning / secret ally prescience | the Nexus player sees the chosen element | when O submits | yes | no |
| 15 | G is aggressor vs defender | no difference | – | – | – |
| 16 | Third parties (not G or G's partner) | must not see the element | – | – | – |
