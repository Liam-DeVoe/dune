
  Faction identity
  - Faction name beside every sigil in prompts, the log, deals, seats, and the lobby, with the player's name where it matters.
  - A "You are playing X" line at game start.

  The prompt card
  - Three states with a kicker line: what phase, who must act, how long is left.
  - The waiting state names who is up and who is next.
  - The action form lives inside the prompt rather than a second panel below it.
  - Auto-continue on by default, with a visible countdown and a pause.
  - A timer bar that goes storm red under ten seconds, visible on the waiting side too.

  Number entry
  - Quick-value buttons: none, max, all, current bid plus one.
  - Bid picker re-syncs to bid plus one when someone outbids you.
  - A running placed-of-total count on force placement.
  - Sectors grouped under their territory instead of one row each.
  - Ship form shows cost per destination and says what you can afford when Ship is disabled.

  Panels and hierarchy
  - Secondary panels collapsed by default, one open lit card at a time.
  - Deals: human offers highlighted and named, bot offers dimmed and collapsed after two, Decline and Counter buttons, Accept labelled with
    the price.
  - Advanced rules grouped by expansion, each collapsible with an on-count, plus a filter box.
  - Host setup explains that picking an expansion faction changes the ruleset, and the seat list says what bots will do.
  - Toolbar icons get correct tooltips and names, chat and users move to a toolbar drawer, skin upload moves into settings.
  - Help panel becomes a titled drawer with search first and links marked in-app or PDF.

  Battle
  - Every plan field always present and labelled, leaders named, live total strength.
  - Result as a one-line banner with drawn wheels carrying faction, leader, and cards, a Close button, and the map dimmed behind.

  Notifications and presence
  - Toasts for incoming deals, chat, and disconnects.
  - Disconnected player shown in the seat list with a countdown to bot takeover.
  - Chat shows sender, faction, and scope, and private messages are labelled.

  Log
  - Sigil, bold name, sentence, with numbers as small tokens, entries about you highlighted, and a this-phase filter.

  Popover
  - Max width so it stops covering the map, a title, and a name beside the occupying sigil.

  Layout and mobile
  - Turn and phase strip above the map, map takes the width the right column wastes.
  - Player names in body type, not the logo font, on the map ring too.
  - Phone: map on top, prompt as a bottom sheet, everything else in tabs, no hover dependence, tap-to-open territory sheet.
  - Wordmark moved above the login form instead of behind it.

  Small copy and polish
  - Non-host trade button mislabelled "Pick Atreides."
  - Ruleset label disagreeing with the report ("Standard Dune" versus "All Expansions").
  - Killed leaders stack unlabelled in the top-left corner.
